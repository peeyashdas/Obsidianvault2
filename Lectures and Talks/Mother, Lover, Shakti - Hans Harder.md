**The women’s issue**

Long literature of modern writing in the Bengali literary landscape into which Tagore enters in the 1900. The corpus of modern Bengali literature has assumed a gigantic shape already by that them - Tagore was writing himself into an existing tradition. He was reacting to the thick cultural atmosphere, on various instances of the women’s question. 

This included the sati debates, harsh criticism of the *Kulin* marriage system, harsh criticism of the child marriage, the “new woman” i.e the *nabina*. The nabina was opposed to the prachina, which was criticised by the traditionalist satirists. Brahmo Samaj was at the flagbearer of this movement, advocated the emancipation of women, who became targets of the conservative satirists, like Jotindranath Sen. 

**Ten Novels**

Written between, 1901 to 1931.

Specific to the upper class, and gender relations, his novels fails to depict the Dalit life and Muslim life. According to Harder, his novels had a polyphony - the capacity of the novel to integrate different voices and perspective under one narrative - the unique feature of novels as mentions by Michael Bakhtin.

POLYPHONY - Polyphony in literature, a term coined by Mikhail Bakhtin, refers to ==a narrative style featuring a diversity of independent, unmerged voices and consciousnesses that coexist without being dominated by a single authorial viewpoint==. It creates a "dialogic" truth where multiple, often conflicting, perspectives are equally valid
