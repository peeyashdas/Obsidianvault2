Traditional Ecological Knowledge

- Local - indigenous knowledge is context specific, has roots in a particular place
- Oral transmission, through imitation and demonstration 
- Social memory - shared to a much greater extent , understanding and knowledge is communal 
- Adaptive capacity - over time and through everyday life experiences, indigenous knowledge is adapted through repetitive learning 
- Holistic - based on numerous interlinked facets of the people’s lives
