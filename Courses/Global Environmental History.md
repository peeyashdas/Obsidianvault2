My Digital Storytelling - How I decided my thesis topic

1. So, my story begins on a sultry evening in Heidelberg, when I was working as a research assistant for a visiting scholar. She wanted me to comb through the national archives of India, and find all the documents related to sound related conflict. So , she suggested that I search keywords like conflict, fight, dispute and nuisance among others. While doing this, I stumbled upon this document titled spring mosquito nuisance and that really intrigued me. So I saved the file in my library to come back to it later.
2. If you grow up in India, mosquito is a crucial part of your everyday experience. We also have deadly diseases like malaria and dengue, and I happened to live in a city that had a dengue outbreak in the aftermath of a flood. So, mosquito as a nuisance was a relatable title, but also quite informal than other documents.
3. This topic is related to healthcare, diseases and epidemic within the larger spectrum of environmental history. Besides history, biology was my favourite subject, so I try to keep them together. 
4. The main thing that interested me was the editing process that was revealed in the documents. 
5. My main attempt at solving it, is by taking it up as my thesis project. 
6. The major obstacle I faced was the absence of secondary literature regarding the main report.
