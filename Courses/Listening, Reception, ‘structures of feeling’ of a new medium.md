This would consider the larger architecture of radio in Weimar era Germany, and draw on the scholarship about radio in Germany in this era. What does radio do which text doesn’t? It would consider the question of what radio can do, what political agency it might have. There is a lot on this second question on Nazi radio, but the trick would be perhaps to acknowledge that radio has this inherent capacity. How many radio listeners were there, Bundes-wide? How many versions of the play can be traced in subsequent years?

Research objectives:
1. Larger architecture of radio in Weimar Republic
2. Draw on scholarship about radio in Germany in this era
3. What does radio do that text does not? Effects of radio on the public 
4. The political agency of radio - its role in Nazi propaganda
5. The inherent capacity of radio as a political agent
6. Reach of radio - how many listeners were there Bundes - wide
7. How many versions of the play can be traced in the subsequent years
8. The radio internationale
